How often things run

Ranked stats are collected every 2 minutes and every tracked player is refreshed on every run. Steam playtime is hourly, because Steam only recomputes the two-week figure a few times a day. Presence polling runs every 5 minutes and exists to estimate hours for players whose Steam playtime is hidden.

This page reads the same published data the board does, so it cannot show a problem the board would hide. It refreshes itself every 30 seconds. The raw figures behind it are at /api/status.
