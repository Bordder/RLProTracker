Privacy policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

This site has no accounts and sets no cookies. The only thing you ever give it is what you type into the feedback form, and that gets published publicly.

1. Who runs this

RL Pro Tracker at 198x.online is an independent fan project run by one person. It makes no money and is not connected to Psyonix, Epic Games, RLCS, Liquipedia or Valve. Use of the site is also covered by the terms.

2. What you give us

Nothing, unless you use the feedback form. There is no sign-up and no login.

If you do send feedback, we get your message and the username you typed, if you typed one. That field is free text and is not checked, so it does not have to be your real name.

Feedback is posted publicly as a GitHub issue, where anyone can read it. Do not put anything private in it. We can delete an issue if you ask, but not copies other people have already made.

3. What gets collected automatically

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and cannot follow you to other sites. It sees the page you loaded, where you came from, and rough details like country and browser. It cannot identify you.

Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs, including IP addresses, because that is how a site gets served and protected. We do not read, export or analyse them.

4. Who else sees it

Nobody. We do not sell or share anything, and there are no ads. Two providers handle data because the site runs on them: Cloudflare hosts it, and GitHub stores the code and the published feedback.

5. Players on the board

Everything shown is already public and is collected without logging into anything. The how it works page explains exactly where each number comes from.

Only RLCS players are listed, and only their competitive activity. If you are one of them and want your row gone, message me and it goes.

6. How long things are kept

Published feedback stays on GitHub until deleted. Collected player data rolls over automatically and anything older than fifteen days is dropped. There is nothing else being kept.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to it being processed, or complain to your national data protection authority.

Here that is simple: unless you have sent feedback, we hold nothing about you. If you have, point me at it and I will delete it.

8. Age

This site is not aimed at children under 13. If you think a child has put personal information in the feedback form, tell me and I will remove it.

9. Changes

Any update appears on this page with a new date at the top.

10. Contact

Discord is the direct route: @_198x. You can also use the feedback form on the front page or open an issue on GitHub, but both of those are public, so use Discord for anything about your own data.
