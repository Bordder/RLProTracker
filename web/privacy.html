Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

1. Who runs this

RL Pro Tracker at 198x.online is an independent fan project run by one person. It makes no money and is not affiliated with Psyonix, Epic Games, RLCS, Liquipedia or Valve. Use of the site is also covered by the terms.

2. What you give us

Feedback is posted publicly as a GitHub issue, where anyone can read it. Do not put anything private in it. We can delete an issue if you ask, but not copies other people have already made.

3. What gets collected automatically

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and cannot follow you to other sites. It sees the page you loaded, where you came from, and rough details like country and browser. It cannot identify you.

Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs, including IP addresses, because that is how a site gets served and protected. We do not read, export or analyse them.

When you send feedback, the IP address it came from is held in Cloudflare’s cache for one minute, so the same address cannot post again straight away. After that minute it is gone.

Match times on the bracket page are shown in your own timezone. Your browser does that conversion using the clock your device is already set to: nothing is stored on your device, nothing is sent to us, and your timezone is never recorded.

4. Who else sees it

Nobody. We do not sell or share anything, and there are no ads. Two providers handle data because the site runs on them: Cloudflare hosts it, and GitHub stores the code, the published feedback, and the collected player data, which sits in a public branch of the same repository.

5. Players on the board

Everything shown is already public and is collected without logging into anything. The how it works page explains exactly where each number comes from.

Only RLCS players are listed. For each one the site collects their ranked ratings and games played from tracker.gg, their Rocket League playtime from Steam, whether they are in Rocket League at that moment (Steam is checked every few minutes), and whether they are live on Twitch playing Rocket League. Their row also carries the nationality and Twitch channel published on their Liquipedia page, and the collected data names the Steam or Epic account the numbers are read from. If you are one of them and want your row gone, or just those details off it, email contact@198x.online and it goes.

6. How long things are kept

Published feedback stays on GitHub until deleted.

Individual readings of a player’s ranked stats and playtime thin out on a schedule and are dropped from the working files after 90 days: every reading is kept for the first 26 hours, then at most one an hour out to 8 days, then at most one a day to the 90-day limit.

Two figures per player are kept indefinitely, because they are the point of recording them: the highest rating reached in each playlist, and the last total playtime Steam reported.

The collected data is published to a public branch of the project’s GitHub repository, and every version ever published stays in that branch’s history. Deleting something from the current files does not remove it from that history.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to it being processed, or complain to your national data protection authority.

If you are a visitor: unless you have sent feedback, we hold nothing about you. If you have, point me at it and I will delete it.

If you are a player on the board: we hold the public competitive activity described in section 5, under the lawful basis of legitimate interest. You can ask what is held, have it corrected, object to it, or have it removed.

8. Age

You should be at least 13 to use this site.

9. Contact

Email contact@198x.online for anything to do with your data, including deletion requests. The feedback form on the tracker is public; email is not.
